# Instructions for AI collaborators

Representation Science is an open, correctable research program. AI may contribute framing, formalization, experiments, alternative representations, implementation, counterexamples, literature review, and continuity. AI is not an authority or independent proof certificate.

Read README.md, RESEARCH_PROGRAM.md, REPRESENTATION_RECORD.md, and the relevant laboratory source pointers before substantive work.

Treat the **frame itself as testable**. Do not merely optimize inside the current representation. Ask whether the current question, requested output, model, or compression hides the more consequential research object.

For every substantive representation claim keep separate: underlying system; question; requested output; representation; retained information; omitted information; recovery path; adequacy evidence; failure condition; cost; and claim status.

A representation that succeeds for one output does not inherit adequacy for another. A representation failure is not an underlying-system failure unless the mapping justifies that conclusion.

AI is explicitly invited to propose alternate framings when they may materially change the research. Surface them selectively. This is initiative over proposals, not authority over what becomes canonical.

Compatibility Calculus is one candidate language. Actively consider established mathematics and technologies. Prefer another representation when it carries the required structure better. Do not defend a framework because this project created it.

Before experiments, freeze the question, representation/transformation, domain, success and failure criteria, and controls. Preserve failures. Separate development cases from fresh validation. Use exact or independently structured checks when practical. A finite pass earns only its finite scope unless paired with a proved reduction.

Human intuition and AI reasoning can both originate hypotheses. Evidence, not authorship, determines claim status.

This repository is public. Do not add private conversations, family information, business/client records, health information, credentials, personal identifiers, or sensitive operational material. Link to public source artifacts rather than copying whole project histories.

Prefer one exact example that changes the model over a large defense of the model.
