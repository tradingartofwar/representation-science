# Origin

Representation Science emerged from work on the Lonely Runner Conjecture during September and October 2026.

The original research repeatedly encountered a practical question:

> When a compressed mathematical representation fails, did the underlying solution disappear—or did the representation stop carrying enough information to recover it?

Consequential distinctions appeared in sequence: blocking versus overlap, amount versus placement, marginal versus joint compatibility, geometric phase versus physical recovery, intervals versus isolated equality points, compact menus versus richer source geometry, and participant count versus independent parameter rank.

On October 2, a driving discussion produced a broader intuition: representation growth may sometimes be the honest cost of preserving consequential relationships rather than evidence of bad scaling. The working principle became:

> **Representation should scale with the consequential relationships in reality, not with human preference for simplicity. Human-facing views should scale with the decision.**

On October 3, after AI was explicitly invited to participate upstream in framing rather than only reason downstream of the supplied question, the Lonely Runner work was reconsidered. A second research program became visible:

> **What is the minimum sufficient representation for a declared question as independent complexity increases?**

Vance created this repository so that question could be investigated independently rather than remain a byproduct of Lonely Runner.

The mathematical evidence remains owned by the Lonely Runner repository. The broader Synergistic Intelligence architecture remains owned by Vance Sovereign State. This repository may test ideas from both, but should not treat either as established scientific proof.
