# Representation Science

**Status:** New open research program, October 3, 2026

## Central question

> **What must a representation preserve for different questions to remain answerable as independent complexity increases?**

Representation Science studies the relationship among reality, representations of reality, the questions asked of those representations, information lost through compression, and the cost of preserving enough structure to remain correctable.

A representation is not good or bad in the abstract. Its adequacy belongs to a question, an operation, a domain, and a consequence.

This work emerged from the [Lonely Runner Conjecture project](https://github.com/tradingartofwar/the-lonely-runner-conjecture), which repeatedly produced the loop:

> **represent → compress → test → expose a lost distinction → enrich or replace → retest**

Examples included blocking versus overlap, amount versus placement, marginal versus joint compatibility, phase versus recoverable physical time, positive-width intervals versus isolated equality points, compact menus versus richer source representations, and participant count versus genuinely independent structure.

Those episodes suggested an independent research object: **representation adequacy itself**.

Lonely Runner remains **Laboratory One**, not the owner of the larger inquiry. Compatibility Calculus is one experimental language, not the definition of Representation Science.

## Working principles

Adequacy is question-specific. Preserve consequential distinctions. Treat representational failure separately from failure of the underlying reality. Compactness is useful only while the representation remains adequate. Separate participant count from independent structure. Preserve a recovery path to richer state. Let reality correct or replace the language.

## Initial research program

The first program is the **Resolution Frontier**. For a fixed underlying system, vary the question:

Does a solution exist? Produce one witness. Determine the optimum. Produce every maximizer. Produce the complete feasible set. Add a genuinely independent constraint and repeat.

For each transition ask: What representation was sufficient before? What information had been omitted? Which omitted distinction becomes necessary now? Can it be recovered? What is the smallest known representation supporting the new operation? What is the cost of preserving optionality for questions not yet asked?

See [Research Program](RESEARCH_PROGRAM.md).

## Relationship to Synergistic Intelligence

The architectural principle that motivated part of this inquiry is:

> **Representation should scale with the consequential relationships in reality, not with human preference for simplicity. Human-facing views should scale with the decision.**

Representation Science asks whether parts of that idea can be made precise, falsifiable, measurable, or mathematically tractable. It does not use the broader architecture as proof.

## Current posture

Do not rush to make Representation Science a grand theory. Build it through exact examples.

The first objective is not to prove that a new field exists. It is to determine whether treating **representation adequacy as an experimental object** produces useful, reproducible knowledge that ordinary problem-specific analysis would otherwise miss.
