# Working note — scientific anomalies as tests of representation and model adequacy

**Date:** November 5, 2026  
**Status:** OPEN / HYPOTHESIS / future-exploration candidate; not an experiment, physics discovery, or new Representation Science result.  
**Home:** Representation Science. Lonely Runner is a mathematical source of the methodological analogy, not a newly established explanation for cosmological or quantum phenomena.

## Generating question

**When a measurement appears to contradict a theory, what exactly has failed: the underlying physical model, a conditional prediction, a measurement or calibration, an inference pipeline, an independence assumption, or the representation through which we learned about the result?**

This question arose from considering popular accounts that group Hubble, structure-growth (S8), muon g-2, and Bell-test claims into a single story of hidden foundational failure. The dramatic story is *hypothesis-generating*; the fact that several anomalies are mentioned together does not establish a common mechanism.

The transferable object is an **anomaly-interpretation contract**, not a claim that physics is wrong or that existing Representation Science predicts a new physical theory.

## Independent research direction — find the first broken bridge

**November 5 refinement after Vance emphasized the assistant's proposed direction rather than the narrator's physics story.** The primary research opportunity is not the proposition that several frontier anomalies share one hidden physical cause. It is a general, falsifiable method for distinguishing an apparent contradiction **between representations** from a contradiction **within the best supported model of reality**.

### The bridge problem

A reported scientific value is often the end of a chain:

\`physical system X → instrument observations D → calibrated observations C → estimated target θ under assumptions M → compressed public claim S\`

Another research group may use a different chain, \`X → D' → C' → θ' under M' → S'\`. Two terminal numbers may disagree even if the instruments are accurate, because \`θ\` and \`θ'\` are different operational targets, use different conditioning assumptions, or have correlated errors. Conversely, two apparently compatible terminal numbers can conceal a real discrepancy if summarization erased relevant scope, time, uncertainty, or dependency.

**Candidate central question:** *Which is the earliest link at which two justified descriptions of one purported target cease to agree, and what minimum additional information is needed to locate that failure?*

This yields a diagnostic posture: **localize before theorizing**. Do not explain an anomaly by modifying physics before establishing whether the purportedly compared quantities are the same estimand, whether their inferential maps are commensurable, and whether shared evidence has been accounted for. Equally, do not “explain away” a reproducible residual merely by naming possible systematics.

### Proposed methodological object: a discrepancy localization record

For each purported conflict, preserve:

- the same-target assertion and its evidence (**are these genuinely estimates of the same thing?**);
- both raw-to-claim transformation chains and model versions;
- an explicit dependency graph for shared inputs/calibrators;
- the point(s) where the chains diverge in definition, assumption, resolution, or observation;
- which candidate repairs are valid on independent grounds, not merely because they reduce the numerical discrepancy;
- whether a residual remains after independently justified revisions;
- a counterfactual test: what should change if this proposed source of disagreement is the real cause?

The output need not be a single “anomaly score.” A **vector of unresolved bridges**—measurement, definition, calibration, inference, dependence, model, and narrative—may be safer and more informative. This overlaps established uncertainty quantification, metrology, causal modeling and model checking; the value of packaging it as Representation Science must be demonstrated against those strong baselines.

### A testable crossover with Lonely Runner

Lonely Runner allows a known underlying mathematical reality and exact checks. Deliberately create two superficially conflicting reports: one based on a representation that omits isolated equality solutions, and one based on exact full-set computation. A superficial anomaly story might conclude the two “theories” disagree. A localization record should discover that they use different support/decoder contracts and recover the omitted witnesses—without reinterpreting this as failure of the conjecture.

Then construct a **true residual control**: a synthetic system where two nominally comparable pipelines disagree because one has an actual declared model misspecification that cannot be repaired by restoring omitted support. The method must distinguish the false anomaly from the real mismatch rather than labeling every discrepancy an artifact.

### What would make this research genuinely useful?

It would have to beat—or at least add a clear, transferable diagnostic to—competent conventional workflows. For a held-out collection of exact synthetic cases and carefully sourced public examples, test whether the proposed record leads analysts to identify the right mismatch layer more accurately, with better-calibrated uncertainty or lower recovery cost. A null result, including “ordinary provenance tables and sound statistics already do this,” is legitimate.

**Most promising independent insight:** A contradiction is never just between two numbers; it is between two *claims that those numbers should agree*. That claimed agreement is itself a representation with assumptions and evidence obligations. We can investigate the adequacy of the **comparison** as an object, not merely the adequacy of each individual model.

## Connection to the existing program

Representation Science asks: *What must a representation preserve for different questions to remain answerable as independent complexity increases?*

The [Lonely Runner laboratory](../labs/lonely-runner/README.md) supplies exact illustrations:

- **Marginal vs joint:** individual projected feasibility does not guarantee all constraints are jointly satisfied.
- **Equality:** a positive-width-only description can miss isolated valid witnesses; apparent absence from a compressed image is not absence from the physical/exact solution.
- **Representation vs reality:** failure of a selected center, source class, menu, or recovery method is not a counterexample to the original runner property.
- **Question-specific adequacy:** existence, a witness, optimum, all maximizers, and the complete feasible set require different outputs and sometimes different evidence.
- **Independence:** additional objects or datasets are not automatically additional independent structure; dependencies matter.

These are **methodological analogies**, not an assertion that cosmological data have the same mathematics as the runner problem. They connect directly to the established [question-quality note](WORKING_NOTE_QUESTION_QUALITY_UPSTREAM_CONTROL_2026_10_04.md), [directional-compression note](WORKING_NOTE_DIRECTIONAL_COMPRESSION_BIAS_2026_10_12.md), and [Representation Record contract](../REPRESENTATION_RECORD.md).

## A separation that can change the conclusion

A reported “tension” should not be represented as a bare pair of numbers. A minimally useful record keeps distinct:

1. **Underlying system:** the physical quantity, domain and conditions.
2. **Raw observables:** what instruments or surveys actually measure.
3. **Transformation/inference:** calibration, likelihood, model assumptions, selection, fit, and prior choices needed to obtain the quoted result.
4. **Prediction:** which model version and parameter assumptions produce the comparison value.
5. **Discrepancy:** residual, uncertainty definition, significance calculation and covariances.
6. **Independent structure:** shared inputs, calibrators, statistical dependence, overlapping data and nuisance parameters.
7. **Question and output:** “Are these two estimates inconsistent?” differs from “Is a physical law invalid?”, “Is a new theory favored?”, and “Are multiple anomalies one mechanism?”
8. **History and status:** effective date of source/model, subsequent recalibration, conflicting analyses, and explicit recheck trigger.
9. **Recovery route:** exact paper/table/code/data, plus what cannot be recovered from the public summary.
10. **Claim status:** observed measurement, derived estimate, model-dependent discrepancy, candidate cause, or experimentally demonstrated mechanism.

A statistically significant residual is not itself an identified physical cause. A model-conditioned inference is not a direct measurement. A change to the comparison model is not a change to the underlying physical event. These distinctions must survive a compact summary if the next decision depends on them.

## Hypotheses and questions for future work

### H1 — A model-conditional discrepancy can be altered by a change in representation without changing the observations

A new calibration, theory input, covariance model, parameterization or data grouping may change the reported size/significance of an anomaly. This is not always an error: improved representation can produce a better warranted estimate.

**Countercheck:** hold raw observations fixed, change one declared inferential choice, and measure which conclusions survive. Include a conventional, source-native pipeline as a baseline. An observed change does not establish the new pipeline is correct.

### H2 — “Several independent anomalies” may be a representational illusion when their inference chains overlap

Data sets, fitted priors, calibrations, nuisance models or selected interpretations may be shared, even when the experiments are nominally separate.

**Countercheck:** draw a provenance/dependency graph. Compare claims of combined evidence using a dependence-aware model against a naive multiplication of independent-looking significances. If truly independent tests remain, preserve that positive evidence rather than explaining it away.

### H3 — Narrative aggregation can hide consequential status differences

A story may juxtapose a live mismatch, a partially disputed tension, an outdated estimate, and an unsupported empirical claim, then compress them to “four independent anomalies.” Every sentence may sound plausible while the resulting inference is unwarranted.

**Countercheck:** freeze one public-source evidence packet and one fixed question. Compare a compact persuasive account, a source-grounded account, and a source-grounded conventional baseline. Score whether readers distinguish *measured*, *model-derived*, *outdated*, *unverified*, and *hypothesized* claims. This may become a negative/positive control for the existing directional-compression-bias proposal, but is not evidence that bias is intentional.

### H4 — A valid scientific anomaly can reveal the right question to ask, not just a missing answer

For example: “Why do these measurements disagree?” may require first asking “Which transformations make their estimates comparable?” “Are the compared quantities operationally identical?” “Are uncertainties and dependencies modeled consistently?” The new framing earns priority only if it changes a testable or consequential step.

**Countercheck:** compare plans based on the original question versus an upstream audit. Do not mistake a longer conceptual discussion for improved inference.

## Suggested first bounded test, if separately selected

Use **synthetic, source-complete examples**, not frontier cosmology as the initial experimental laboratory.

- **Case A: equality/support loss.** Reuse a published exact Lonely Runner fixture to contrast a full safe-set representation with a positive-width-only summary. Fix the question: existence of a valid witness versus enumeration of all witnesses. Show concretely which claim is supported or lost without suggesting the runner theorem failed.
- **Case B: false independence.** Construct three synthetic observations with a declared common calibration error and a held-out exact truth. Compare naive independent-error aggregation with a dependency-preserving representation. A good test should also include a case with genuinely independent errors and a genuine shared-model discrepancy, so the method cannot “explain away” every anomaly.
- **Case C: evolving source.** Preserve two successive, source-dated versions of a theoretical comparison while holding raw measurement constant. Ask whether a frozen summary silently presents yesterday's model as today's evidence; test whether explicit source/version tracking triggers a correction.
- **Case D: narrative adequacy.** Compare compact scientific summaries on a fixed question and blind reader judgments to see whether omitted status labels change the conclusion.

**Freeze before execution:** input state, question, representation alternatives, exact outputs, recovery privileges, control/baseline, resource ceiling, expected counterexamples, stopping conditions, and how to record null/negative results.

A useful null result would show that a simple conventional source table and covariance-aware analysis already solve the proposed task as well as a new “anomaly audit” representation. That is a legitimate outcome. Do not claim a new mathematical result, general theory, or incremental AI advantage without independent evidence.

## An operational hypothesis: anomaly lifecycle

Anomaly status might use a versioned sequence such as:

**reported → source located → methods checked → reproduced within scope / not reproduced → alternative explanations assessed → independently challenged → revised / unresolved / superseded.**

This is an evidence-workflow proposal, **not** a maturity scale for nature. A discrepancy can persist while its interpretation changes. Superseded records should remain visible as dated history, with downstream summaries corrected when they relied on an obsolete interpretation. No monitoring or automatic propagation is implemented by this note.

This may offer a concrete transfer from mathematical representation adequacy to scientific literature synthesis; whether it adds value over ordinary reproducible research practice remains OPEN.

## What I would not do

- Treat physics anomalies as mutually reinforcing merely because a speaker groups them.
- Assume a common quantum-gravity explanation; require a quantitative mechanism and discriminating predictions first.
- Claim verified “superquantum” Bell violations without specific experimental papers, analysis and independent scrutiny.
- Treat model inadequacy as physical lawlessness, or a failure of a particular representation as failure of the underlying system.
- Start a new major laboratory or rerun frozen Lonely Runner/EXP-002 experiments without their own scope, approval, and integrity requirements.
- Publish excerpts from private conversations or uncited audio/video transcriptions in this public repository.

## Return condition

Revisit when one of the following occurs: (1) a concrete, checkable example shows an inference lost under a declared compression; (2) a representation question in Lonely Runner benefits from a dependency/provenance audit; (3) a reputable new physics result changes one of the dated status summaries; or (4) the researcher selects a bounded synthetic experiment with frozen controls.

**Provisional takeaway:** *An anomaly should carry its inferential lineage. A mismatch between two representations can be a useful clue without being evidence that reality violates a law. Preserve what differs, what remains jointly compatible, and which observation could discriminate the explanations.*
