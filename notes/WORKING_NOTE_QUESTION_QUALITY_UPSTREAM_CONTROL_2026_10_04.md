# Working note — question quality as an upstream control

**Date:** 2026-10-04  
**Status:** WORKING PRINCIPLE / hypothesis for research practice

## Core idea

Representation Science has repeatedly shown that the question determines which distinctions become consequential.

That creates an upstream obligation:

> **Before optimizing the answer or the representation, ask whether we are asking the best question for the present purpose, consequence and reality.**

This does not mean searching for the most profound question. It means checking whether the current question is the one that most directly addresses the actual problem.

## Three levels of quality

1. **Answer quality** — are we answering the stated question well?
2. **Representation quality** — are we preserving the distinctions needed to answer it?
3. **Question quality** — is the stated question actually the right question to organize attention and evidence?

The third level is upstream of the first two.

A system can produce a precise answer from an adequate representation and still fail practically if the question itself is misframed.

## Examples from current work

### Paint

Initial framing:

> What color do I remember the house being?

Operationally better framing:

> What information do I need to match the paint reliably?

The second question changed the required evidence and made direct source comparison relevant.

### Memory

Initial framing:

> How does my memory work?

Operationally better framing:

> What becomes available under different cues, what appears spontaneously versus after prompting, and which parts can be externally checked?

The second question produced cleaner observations and reduced introspective invention.

### EXP-001

The same Lonely Runner reality supported different representational obligations depending on the question:

- existence;
- one witness;
- optimum;
- every maximizer;
- complete safe set.

This is direct evidence that the question is part of the adequacy contract.

## Partnership implication

Vance's authorization for AI to participate in framing should include a standing background check:

> **Are we solving the problem as stated, or has the work exposed a better question?**

The AI should not constantly interrupt with alternate framings. Relevance and consequence still govern surfacing. But when a different question would materially change what evidence, distinctions or actions matter, it should be proposed explicitly.

## Guardrails

- Do not treat every alternate framing as superior.
- Do not replace the human's purpose with an AI-generated purpose.
- Preserve the original question when provenance matters.
- Distinguish a better question from a merely broader or more interesting one.
- Prefer questions that improve contact with reality, reduce conflation, or better match the actual consequence.
- When changing the question changes the research object, version the transition rather than pretending the original question was always wrong.

## Working principle

> **Question quality is an upstream control variable for representation adequacy.**

A better question can change:
- what information is relevant;
- what resolution is required;
- what source must be consulted;
- what counts as evidence;
- what distinctions must survive compression;
- what action follows.

This principle should remain active in both Representation Science and the broader Synergistic Intelligence partnership.
